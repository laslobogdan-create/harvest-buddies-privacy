Harvest Buddies

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 14, 2026

Harvest Buddies is a farming simulation game developed by Vasili Zaitev. This Privacy Policy explains how information is processed when playing the game.

1. Information We Collect

Harvest Buddies uses Google Firebase to provide anonymous authentication and cloud-based game progress storage.

  • Player nickname selected by the player.
  • Saved game progress, including gameplay achievements, inventory, and virtual resources.
  • Anonymous user identifier generated by Firebase Authentication.

Firebase may also process technical information, such as IP addresses and device-related data, to operate its services.

Players are not required to provide their real name, email address, or password.

2. How We Use Information

We use this information to maintain anonymous player profiles, save and restore game progress, provide gameplay functionality, and protect the reliability and security of the game.

3. Third-Party Services

Harvest Buddies uses Google Firebase, including Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore.

For more information, see Firebase Privacy Information and Google's Privacy Policy.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell player information. Information is processed by Google Firebase to provide authentication and game storage.

The game does not currently use advertising or analytics SDKs.

5. Data Security

We use reasonable security measures, including Firebase security features and access controls, to protect stored information. No electronic storage method is completely secure.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Player data is retained as needed to provide game functionality, unless deleted or otherwise required by applicable law.

Players can request deletion of their nickname, anonymous authentication identifier, and saved game progress by contacting laslo.bogdan@gmail.com.

Valid deletion requests will be processed in accordance with applicable law.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the game's features, services, or data practices change. Updates will be published on this page.
